That one sentence covers a lot of ground. Is last year’s exam fair game? Does rewording a source count as plagiarism? Do you have to report a classmate?

Most integrity problems start with confusion, not bad intent. The assistant answers those questions before they turn into cases.

Start with a common question, or ask your own.

  • YouWhat counts as a violation?
  • AssistantCheating, plagiarism, stealing, lying, and tolerating a violation you know about. Section 4 lists each one, and your professor’s syllabus sets what resources are allowed.
  • YouIf I know a classmate cheated, do I have to report it?
  • AssistantYes. Failing to report a known or suspected violation is itself a violation under the Code.
